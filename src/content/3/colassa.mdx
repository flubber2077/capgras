So what I’ve grown fifty feet\
since you last saw me and my feet 

are just a bit-ish filled with fifty\
silver fishes of eight? 

You see I’ve found new ways to escalate. 

Excelsior, darling, for I’ve still one pleasure,\
one true pleasure like one rogue bone —

a wandering coccyx shocking\
each inch of my inside. 

The rooster searches for his plumage\
up my skirts. The sun

in a sundress chafes his fat thighs.\
He’d like to treat me like a drum but

he slides light off my bronze fondue\
into a binge of blue

where beribboned fishbones\
coxcomb thou-sand-thru 

silver waves. A stormy door\
swing-dances between my shanks.

The only way past me is you.
