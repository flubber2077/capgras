I’m not a rare beauty eating a peach beside some blushing roses
“A beauty is a princess because a princess is very special” my daughter (3) says when asked to define it 
Another definition (around Halloween): “a mummy is an invisible person” 
I heard “mommy” & nodded stunned nodded yes, yes unseen
Seen & not heard is how my children relate to me mostly
Mostly others also, even grown-ups, with the children around
Around my students, undergrads, the same thing—I talk talk talk & they nod nod yet when asked a question they say excuse me, what, can you repeat that 
 A mime traces the edges of a secret box
(suggestively) 
May I suggest a change of course—the box, for instance, is a lovely, slow-growing hedge ideal for enclosing property so gradually not even observant neighbors will notice it’s happening till your house is hidden from view
