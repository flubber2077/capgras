The second time pissing in as many weeks\
She sought to remove all traces of the poetry\
from our prenups\
Namely, a sense of homesickness\
welcoming winter like a missionary at the door\
I spent one long year riding the backbench\
connected to all the others who heard the very same chimes\
of worldbuilding and fabulism\
but who couldn’t make out the meaning of the words\
or whom the vector might be\
This is what you get\
sleeping through New Year’s on your sectional\
My subversion was to lay atop the coverlet and kick my little feet\
It put me in a delightful mood
