// [18 October 2024] 
 
How about this.\
I caught this morning morning’s\
cotton candy sky, a floss-spun fog\
to boot. So when I tell you\
I don’t want any hint\
of bitterness I mean it. I mean\
I gotta watch for the grumps,\
the glooms, since resentment\
gets me nothing and gives me less.\
But even then\
I took my coffee with cream\
and sugar, a rare and sickly\
sweet treat, so naturally\
I come around\
to thinking of pineapples\
and that old\
piece of advice, a real head-\
canon, hazarded\
with a knowing wink: to ward\
off bitterness and turn toward\
the way of sugar, to give in\
to that craving above all\
else, or else, to heal\
my nettle-stung nethers months\
after, to cure my honey-\
tinged poison tongue and earn\
back a touch, your touch, of\
all your going graces, I mean, come on.\
How else would I want it, less bitterness,\
less backpay, just you, this, more.\
My drive sharpens as the mini-van\
drives straight down the stretch\
between Cleveland and Jefferson:\
you can piece it together, triangulate\
my position, and even\
now you’re at therapy for my mess\
and my session\
is coming in about\
an hour’s time but\
you know how I focus\
with endgame precision\
on the moment and what’s\
here because I have to, and I’m grateful,\
really, drizzled in caramel, flammable\
still post-offering, sacrifice,\
so this is\
my fragrance at its innermost, this head-\
space a fitting sanctum and most\
of all the best gift, this present, this,\
right here, the cloying, lip-\
smacking transience.
